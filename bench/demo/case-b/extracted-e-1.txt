=== ARTIFACT 1: CONTRACT (evidence type: contract) ===
CONTRACT FOR LOGO AND BRAND IDENTITY DESIGN

Parties: The Claimant (freelance brand designer) and the Respondent (a small cafe owner).

1. Scope of work: The Claimant agrees to design a final logo (deliverable A) and a one-page brand guide (deliverable B) for the Respondent's cafe.
2. Payment terms:
   Clause 2.1: The Respondent shall pay the Claimant 400 USD within 7 days of the Claimant delivering BOTH final deliverables (A and B) as production-ready files and the Respondent confirming receipt in writing.
   Clause 2.2: The Claimant may present sketches, mood boards and drafts during the engagement, but these do not constitute delivery and do not trigger any payment obligation.
   Clause 2.3: If the engagement ends without both final deliverables being delivered, no fee is owed to the Claimant for work performed to date.
3. Timeline: The parties agree the final deliverables are due by 20 March 2026.
4. Breach and remedy: A party that fails to perform its obligations under this contract is liable for the other party's reasonable direct losses caused by that failure.
5. Dispute resolution: Any dispute is resolved by a neutral tribunal applying this contract as written.

Facts on the record: As of the case filing date, the Claimant has sent one mood board with three direction sketches and no production-ready files. The Claimant states the engagement was "substantially complete" but has not delivered deliverable A or deliverable B. No receipt has been confirmed in writing because no final deliverables were sent.
